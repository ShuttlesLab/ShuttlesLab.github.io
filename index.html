About Us

ShuttlesLab is a research group based at the School of Intelligence Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen), led by Prof. Ying Zhang. We conduct fundamental and applied research in three directions: spacecraft attitude & orbit control, intelligent manipulator control, and control-theoretic methods.

In recent years, the team has published in IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, Acta Astronautica and at conferences such as ECC and CAC, undertaken and participated in research projects including NSFC grants, and collaborated with leading universities and research institutions at home and abroad.

Research Directions

Fundamental theories and key technologies of intelligent aerospace systems and robotics

Spacecraft Attitude & Orbit Control

Spacecraft attitude maneuver control, orbit dynamics and guidance, fault-tolerant attitude control and prescribed performance control.

Intelligent Manipulator Control

Coupled dynamics modeling, motion planning, multi-robot coordination and intelligent control of space manipulators and robotic systems.

Control-Theoretic Methods

Robust control and filtering for stochastic and switched systems, iterative algorithms for Riccati equations, reinforcement-learning control and data-driven modeling.
