Our Advisors

Prof. Ying Zhang
Lab Director | Professor, PhD Advisor, School of Intelligence Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen)

Ph.D. in Guidance, Navigation and Control from Harbin Institute of Technology (2007), and has visited the University of Western Australia, the Hong Kong Polytechnic University and the University of Minnesota. Research interests include spacecraft attitude maneuver control, robust control and filtering for stochastic and switched systems. First/corresponding author of 30+ SCI papers (including Automatica and IEEE TAC), PI of 10+ funded projects including NSFC grants, author of one English and one Chinese monograph, holder of 10+ national invention patents. Secretary-General of the Technical Committee on Fully Actuated System Theory and Applications, Chinese Association of Automation.
